# Arena: the rifle becomes an smg (addendum)

**Status:** the user asked on 2026-10-14: "the rifle needs more spray and then less spray when crouched.
also should be like an smg so smaller and do less damage". This addendum amends the PvP spec and the
crouch addendum (`2026-10-14-arena-crouch.md` §3).

- **The name.** The rules, the wire (`w: 'r'`), the level's `R` pickup and the code keep the key `rifle`, so
  old pages and the server still agree. The HUD calls it `smg` (`GUN_NAME`).
- **Smaller.** It is drawn as `╦╤═` (3 glyphs, the hand on the `╦`), down from `═╦╤══`. Its muzzle is at
  `MUZZLE_R.rifle` = 3.8 (was 4.8).
- **Less damage.** 6 a hit (was 9), so a headshot does 12. Rate, speed, range, cap (7), ammo (40), knock and
  stun are unchanged.
- **Spray.** `GUNS.rifle.spray` = `[0.15, 0.04]` radians. Each shot leaves up to that far off the aim,
  either way, at random:
  - 0.15 standing or in the air;
  - 0.04 crouched;
  - prone, the crawl's own spread applies instead (up to 0.5 above, never below level).
  - The deagle and the sniper have no spray.
  - As with the crawl, the spray is rolled on the client and sent as the shot's angle, which the server
    takes as given.
- **Tests:** `sprayOf` and the damage (`test/fight.test.js`); the sprayed angles standing and crouched, and
  none for the other guns (`test/pvp.test.js`); the art and muzzle constants (`test/stick.test.js`); `smg`
  in the HUD (`test/fighthud.test.js`); the 12 headshot (`test/referee.test.js`).
